**3. What is a potential change you'd make to the approach taken in your current Quarter 1 Project?**

One potential change I would make is to introduce more diverse environmental conditions. Our current approach has primarily focused on establishing basic drone navigation and control before gradually introducing more complex wind disturbances and more complex scenarios. While this approach helps us develop a working baseline, introducing a wider variety of wind conditions earlier could help identify potential weaknesses in the controller and improve its robustness. I would also consider evaluating the controller more consistently throughout training to better track its progress, identify areas where it struggles, and determine what improvements could be made to its overall performance.

**4. What other techniques would you be interested in using in your project?**

Another technique I would be interested in using in our project is domain randomization, where the drone would be trained under a variety of randomly generated wind speeds, directions, and disturbances to improve its ability to handle unfamiliar environmental conditions. I would also be interested in experimenting with sensor fusion, which involves combining information from multiple onboard sensors, such as cameras and IMUs, to help the drone better understand its surroundings and respond more effectively to changing conditions.
